# POLAR: A Corpus of Questions, Responses and Argumentation in Polish Political Radio Discourse

This repository contains the POLAR corpus accompanying our paper published at LREC 2026.

## Dataset

POLAR is a Polish-language corpus developed to investigate question–answer structures and their relationship to argumentation in political interviews.

The corpus comprises:
- 21 political radio interviews from RMF FM, Radio ZET, and Polskie Radio;
- 8,980 segments;
- 28 speakers;
- Gold-standard annotations identifying questions, different types of responses, premises, and conclusions.

The annotations include 1,267 interrogative sentences, 1,015 information questions, 336 explicit direct answers, 533 explicit non-direct answers, 257 premises, and 150 conclusions.

## Repository contents

The `data` directory contains the annotated corpus in JSON format.

The dataset includes interview metadata, source references, transcribed segments, speaker information, gold-standard annotations, and the individual annotation records preserved in the dataset.

## Language and sources

The corpus is in Polish. It contains transcriptions of political radio interviews from Polish broadcasters. References to the original recordings and other available source materials are provided in the dataset metadata.
